Terms and Conditions

These terms apply when you use FindBack to report or search for lost and found items. By creating an account or signing in and selecting the agreement box, you agree to these terms.

Using FindBack

Use FindBack only for genuine lost and found reports connected to the university community. You must provide accurate information and update or resolve a report when it is no longer relevant.

Your account and contact details

Keep your sign-in credentials private. Your name, email address, and optional phone number are stored in your FindBack profile. When you submit a report, the contact details in your profile are attached to that report so another signed-in user can contact you about it.

Do not submit another person's contact information or sensitive personal information without their permission. You can update your profile details from the profile menu at the top of the page.

Photos and report content

Only upload photos you have permission to share. Do not upload illegal, misleading, abusive, or unrelated content. FindBack may remove reports that misuse the service.

Safety and item handover

FindBack helps people make contact; it does not verify item ownership or arrange handovers. Use care when contacting others, avoid sharing extra sensitive information, and arrange returns in a safe public place or through university staff.

Service availability

Reports are provided by users and may be incomplete or outdated. FindBack cannot guarantee that an item will be found, returned, or available, or that the service will always be uninterrupted.

Questions

For help with FindBack, contact findback@goa.paruluniversity.ac.in.
